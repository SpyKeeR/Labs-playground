Incomplete Lab - Evaluate the IoT Security Risk in an Industry Sector

Objectives

In this lab, you will research an industry sector and evaluate the risks associated with implementing an IoT solution.

  • Part 1: Research Risks for an Industry Sector
  • Part 2: Investigate the Potential Impact of a Security Breach
  • Part 3: Describe Security Measures for Your Industry Sector

Background / Scenario

Any implementation of IoT is going to have risk associated with it. Risks could range from insignificant to very destructive depending on the market, equipment, number of people affected, and many other factors.

In this lab, you will research an industry sector and evaluate the risks associated with implementing an IoT solution. One of the following industry sectors will be assigned to you.

  • agriculture
  • automotive
  • banking
  • construction
  • education
  • healthcare
  • manufacturing
  • mining
  • retail
  • technology industries
  • utilities

Required Resources

  • Computer with Internet access to be used for research

Part 1: Research Risks for an Industry Sector

In Part 1, you will research your industry sector to determine the risks posed by a security breach. The questions will guide through some of the things you should look for when determining the severity of an IoT security breach. There are many other questions that could be asked to determine risk. Try to formulate other questions that would be important to determine risk.
What types of communications services or protocols are used for connecting IoT devices in your industry sector?
Answer Area
Answers will vary but may include: Wired or wireless Ethernet, serial communication, I2C, Bluetooth, cellular, http, and https.
What are some of the known vulnerabilities?
Answer Area
Answers will vary but may include: With WiFi weak encryption, http is plain text, Bluetooth can be highjacked, serial communication can be tapped into, Ethernet traffic in general can be sniffed and captured.

Answer Area
Answers will vary but may include: Altered data such as health readings could have posed a life-threatening situation. Data such as storm information could cause panic.
What types of equipment are being monitored or managed?
Answer Area

Part 2: Investigate the Potential Impact of a Security Breach

In this part, use the information gathered from your research above to answer the following questions:
What is the security risk with the data being compromised?
Answer Area
Answers will vary based on industry.
Is the security risk life threatening? In what way is safety impacted?
Answer Area
Answers will vary but may include: Healthcare device tampering has the potential to be life threatening. Tampering with things like the electrical grid could have a major impact on many peoples’ lives.
Does the security risk pose a financial loss? Describe the financial impact.
Answer Area
Most any security breach has some financial impact. Loss could stem from lost time, damaged equipment or confidential data.
Does the security risk expose personal identity information? What types of information might be exposed?
Answer Area
If the breach lets the attacker gain internal access it is possible that PII may be compromised. The Target breach exposed millions of individuals.
